# 🤝 Contributing to NASA Space Apps Guide

Thank you for your interest in contributing!
We welcome everyone who wants to make this guide more useful for NASA Space Apps Challenge participants.

## How Can You Contribute?

- Improve existing documentation.
- Add useful NASA resources and APIs.
- Share tutorials and learning materials.
- Fix grammatical errors or outdated information.
- Add beginner-friendly explanations.

## How to Contribute

1. Fork this repository.
2. Clone your fork.
3. Create a new branch.
4. Make your changes.
5. Commit your changes.
6. Open a Pull Request.

You can also open an Issue to suggest improvements.

## Guidelines

- Keep explanations beginner-friendly.
- Verify information before submitting.
- Prefer official NASA resources.
- Include references and sources.
- Avoid plagiarism.
- Respect third-party licenses.

## Community Guidelines

Be respectful, welcoming, and constructive.
Every contribution matters. Thank you for helping make space exploration more accessible!

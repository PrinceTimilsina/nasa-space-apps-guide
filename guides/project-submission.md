# Project Submission Guide

Your project must be submitted through the official NASA Space Apps platform, not just uploaded to GitHub. GitHub is useful for collaboration and sharing code, but it does not replace the official submission.

## Before submission

- Read the current year's official Project Submission Guide.
- Confirm the submission deadline and time zone.
- Check which team member is responsible for submitting.
- Confirm the project title and challenge selection.
- Prepare a concise problem statement and solution description.
- Explain which NASA or partner data you used and how.
- Prepare screenshots, diagrams, a demo, or a video if the current form requests them.
- Check that all external resources are credited.
- Make sure your public links work and do not expose secrets or private information.

## Explain your project clearly

A useful project explanation usually covers:

1. **Problem:** What challenge are you addressing?
2. **Audience:** Who could benefit from the solution?
3. **Solution:** What did your team create or propose?
4. **Data:** Which datasets or APIs did you use?
5. **Method:** How does your prototype work?
6. **Impact:** What could it help people understand or do?
7. **Limitations:** What does it not do yet?
8. **Next steps:** What would you improve with more time?

Use the exact fields and word limits in the current official submission form.

## Demo and presentation

- Show the core idea early.
- Use a short, reliable demonstration.
- Explain the connection between the challenge and your solution.
- Make charts and visuals readable.
- Do not claim that a prototype is proven or operational if it has not been validated.
- Prepare a backup recording or screenshots in case the live demo fails, if permitted by the event rules.

## Final review checklist

- [ ] Correct team and challenge selected.
- [ ] Required form fields completed.
- [ ] Required media or links included.
- [ ] Data sources and third-party assets credited.
- [ ] Links tested in a signed-out/private browser window.
- [ ] No passwords, API keys, or private information exposed.
- [ ] Submission reviewed by the team.
- [ ] Official confirmation of submission received.

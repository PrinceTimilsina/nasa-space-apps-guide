# Registration Guide

Registration steps and deadlines can change each year. Use this page as a general checklist and confirm every detail on the official NASA Space Apps website.

## General registration checklist

1. Visit https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/
2. Create an account or sign in using the options shown on the official website.
3. Find the current year's NASA Space Apps Challenge.
4. Read the participant terms, privacy information, eligibility requirements, and event instructions.
5. Complete the official event registration form.
6. Choose an available local event or virtual participation option, if offered.
7. Check your account or confirmation message to make sure registration is complete.
8. Save important dates and check official announcements for updates.

## Local event or virtual participation

The official website may list in-person local events and virtual participation options. Availability, capacity, location rules, and selection deadlines vary. Choose only an option that is actually available to you and follow its instructions.

## Information to prepare

The registration form may ask for details such as your name, contact information, location, skills, or event preference. Only enter information on the official website and provide only what is requested.

## If something goes wrong

- Re-check that you are signed in to the correct account.
- Read the error message carefully.
- Check the official FAQ and help information.
- Contact the event organizers through the contact method listed on the official website.
- Do not share your password or verification codes with anyone.

# Team Formation Guide

A good team combines useful skills, communicates clearly, and agrees on a realistic goal. You do not need a team made up entirely of programmers.

## Skills that can help

- **Research:** understands the challenge, finds reliable sources, and checks assumptions.
- **Science or subject knowledge:** helps interpret the problem and data.
- **Development:** builds the website, app, model, or prototype.
- **Design:** plans the user experience, visuals, and accessibility.
- **Data analysis:** cleans, explores, and visualizes datasets.
- **Communication:** writes the story, prepares the demo, and presents the project.
- **Coordination:** tracks tasks, decisions, and deadlines.

One person can cover more than one area, and roles can change as the project develops.

## Finding teammates

- Use the official Space Apps platform and event community features where available.
- Attend official local-event or virtual meetups.
- Ask classmates, clubs, educators, or community groups.
- Explain what skills you have and what kind of teammate you are looking for.
- Follow the official participant rules when contacting other participants.

## Agree on these things early

1. Which challenge are we addressing?
2. Who is the intended user or beneficiary?
3. What is our smallest useful prototype?
4. Which tools and communication channel will we use?
5. Who owns each task?
6. When will we check progress?
7. How will we make decisions if we disagree?
8. Who will submit the project and verify that all team details are correct?

## A simple task board

| Task | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Understand the challenge | Research lead | To do |
| Find and inspect data | Data lead | To do |
| Sketch the solution | Design lead | To do |
| Build a prototype | Development lead | To do |
| Test and document | Whole team | To do |
| Prepare presentation | Presentation lead | To do |

## Teamwork tips

- Set small tasks with clear outcomes.
- Share progress regularly.
- Keep important decisions in writing.
- Ask for help early when blocked.
- Credit everyone's contributions.
- Be respectful and inclusive.

## Team size and platform rules

Team-size limits, team-formation dates, and rules for joining or changing teams can vary by year. Check the current official participant guide and team page. Do not rely on an old blog post for current requirements.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need to be a programmer?

No. Space Apps welcomes participants with different backgrounds and skills. Research, science, design, writing, storytelling, and communication can all contribute to a team.

## Do I need a team before registering?

Registration and team-formation processes can vary by year. Check the current official website for the sequence, deadlines, and available ways to find teammates.

## Can I participate remotely?

Virtual participation options depend on the current year's event setup and available local events. Check the official event listing for the options available to you.

## Where do I find the challenges?

Use the challenge listings on the official NASA Space Apps website. Read the full challenge statement and linked resources rather than relying only on summaries.

## Where can I find NASA data?

Start with [NASA Open Data](https://data.nasa.gov/), [NASA APIs](https://api.nasa.gov/), [NASA Earthdata](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/), and the relevant NASA science or mission archive.

## Do I need to use a NASA API?

Not necessarily. A project may use a dataset, a data archive, an API, or another permitted resource. Choose the data source that genuinely supports your solution and follow its terms.

## Does creating a GitHub repository submit my project?

No. GitHub is useful for storing code and documentation. You must submit through the official Space Apps platform and follow the current submission instructions.

## Can I work on a project after the hackathon?

You can continue developing your own work, subject to the participant terms, intellectual-property rules, and any third-party licenses that apply. Read the current official terms.

## Is this repository official NASA guidance?

No. It is an independent community-created guide. The official NASA Space Apps website, participant terms, and current-year guides take precedence.

## Where can I find the latest rules and dates?

Always check https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/ and the current year's official resources. Rules, schedules, team limits, eligibility, and submission requirements may change.
